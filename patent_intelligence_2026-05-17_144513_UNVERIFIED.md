### Apparent Landscape Summary

The local patent excerpts reveal a landscape with multiple clusters: OLED-based photobiomodulation devices from Korean academic and corporate entities, wearable photobiomodulation systems in various form factors, and transcranial near-infrared therapy for neurological applications. ### Apparent Theme Clusters

- **OLED-based photobiomodulation devices**  _(refs: L9-L12, L45-L48, L29-L31)_
    These patents explicitly describe organic light-emitting diodes (OLEDs) for photobiomodulation, including bio-OLEDs and tandem structures, covering red and NIR emission for therapeutic applications. Assignees include DSLAB, Sungkyunkwan University, KAIST, and Seoul National University Bundang Hospital.
- **Wearable photobiomodulation systems**  _(refs: L17-L19, L20-L24, L49-L52, L40-L44)_
    These patents disclose wearable form factors such as masks, combs, sports cups, and head-worn devices that deliver red and/or NIR light for PBM. They often integrate sensors or massage functions. Assignees include SHINING BUDDHA CORP., MEDIFY, INC., and Chinese companies.
- **Transcranial and neurological photobiomodulation**  _(refs: L32-L34, L53-L57)_
    These patents focus on transcranial delivery of near-infrared light for neurological conditions such as depression, sleep induction, and cognitive enhancement. JelikaLite LLC and TEDI MEDI CO., LTD. are prominent applicants.
- **General photobiomodulation therapy systems**  _(refs: L25-L28, L35-L39)_
    These patents describe broader PBM therapy systems using LED arrays, often modular and configurable. Joovv, Inc. is a key applicant, with patents covering systems and methods for delivering red and NIR light to the body.
